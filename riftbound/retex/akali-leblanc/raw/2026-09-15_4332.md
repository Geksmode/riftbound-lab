## Extraits
- However, when bans happen, I can't help but wonder who's next in line to become the dominant synergy. This time around, I'm afraid the answer is a bit too obvious: The Calm domain. Among the top performers behind Kennen heading into those bans, Master Yi, Irelia, Akali and Azir are some of the first legends most people would mention. An entire domain is much better than a single legend, especially as these four don't necessarily play the same way nor share the same second domain. Yet, beating a domain is also much more difficult than targeting a specific legend.
- Kennen has been the best legend in Riftbound for most of Vendetta. Baring the early weeks while everyone was figuring out how to build each legend, the Heart of the Tempest has been the most popular and best performing legend in both third-party and regional events. However, something Kennen never managed to achieve is to win a Regional. Indeed, the legend made it to the finals on three occasions, but lost to Ornn, Irelia and Akali. You might notice something, these three all share the same domain: Calm.
- Arguably, Ornn won Barcelona on the back of a fantastic metagame call mixed with a list nobody saw coming. However, Irelia wasn't an overlooked legend when she won in Wuhan, while Akali might have been in Singapore but definitely wasn't in Shenyang. To those three, we can also add Master Yi and Azir, two legends with several top 8 under their belt in Vendetta, and maybe Vex who just go her first one last weekend.
- Astral Heron (VEN-044) is arguably the most important card for Akali, Irelia or Vex at the moment, massively improving those Calm legends ability to hold in the later stages of a match.
- As the sole Calm legend able to run Decree of Rage (VEN-015) , does the two time regional winner Akali get even better with these two bans?
