## Extraits
- In a repeat of Barcelona and Wuhan, Kennen once again lost in the finals in Singapore on September 6th. This time, the surprise challenger was Akali, a legend on the rise over the past three regionals, after being considered a weak release early in Vendetta. Here is the tournament report and the latest decks from the event.
- Through locking half the top 8 spots alongside an undefeated run in the swiss rounds, Kennen once again proved he is the best legend in Riftbound at the moment. However, even if this is the best performance by the Heart of the Tempest so far in Vendetta, most of the community will remember one thing: Kennen lost to Akali in the finals.
- That same Rogue Assassin everyone considered a weak legend heading into Barcelona. There, a couple of top 16 with a list built around Astral Heron (VEN-044) showed Akali had some potential. The following week in Wuhan, a similar deck reached the top 8 to cement that legend as a solid competitive option in high-stakes tournaments. Today in Singapore, Akali went all the way with an updated list around that same core, featuring Blitzcrank, Impassive (OGN-067) as the main innovation.
- Overall, I read this as a metagame with many competitive legends able to contend for a top 8 spot in a 2,000 players contest. However, there is one big tree named Kennen, and a smaller one named Master Yi hiding the rest of the forest. These two combine to create the same puzzle we had to solve in Unleashed, with Diana and that same Master Yi. Combined, they took 13 of the 24 spots in regional tournament top 8s, enough to call them a dominant duo. Enough to hide the potential of Akali, Azir, Rengar, Fiora and such capable legends, also able to reach the same competitive highs. Yet, these two also add up to zero regional win, as that same group of capable legends managed to beat Kennen in each of the three finals we had so far.
