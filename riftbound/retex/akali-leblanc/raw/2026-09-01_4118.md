- Akali is the only legend on this list with both a regular and Signature variant showing up in the price rankings, which gives you a clear look at the gap between the two.
- The most important thing to understand about this list is that the variant type is what's driving the price, not the card itself. The base versions of all these legend cards cost almost nothing: Kennen's regular card is $0.11, Akali's is $0.13, Renekton's is $0.10. The only reason any of these are in the hundreds or thousands is because they are Overnumbered or artist-signed variants.
- Within that context, character popularity and meta performance explain why some Signatures cost more than others. Akali's artist-signed version sitting above Kennen's despite a lower win rate comes down to character demand and how the artwork landed with collectors. Kennen holding above Nasus reflects both meta dominance and broader appeal. But none of these cards would be on this list at all without the variant rarity, artwork, and signature doing the heavy lifting first.
