## Extraits
- With a second win for Astral Heron (VEN-044) at the top of a midrange deck, this time alongside Akali rather than Irelia, the Calm Domain once again proved it was Kennen's biggest foe in the Vendetta metagame. Overall, through locking half the top 8 spots alongside an undefeated run in the swiss rounds, Kennen once again proved he is the best legend in Riftbound at the moment. However, even if this is the best performance by the Heart of the Tempest so far in Vendetta, most of the community will remember one thing: Kennen lost in the finals once more.
- In Barcelona, it was to a surprisingly effective Ornn Aggro deck, kicking off the narrative around fast-paced decks being the best way to tackle this metagame. In Wuhan, Irelia showed midrange decks were capable of fighting back, thanks to Astral Heron (VEN-044) allowing them to hold a battlefield late in a match. Singapore felt like a sort of a repeat of the Wuhan open, with Akali replacing Irelia while the other actors played a similar role.

- Tier 3 LeBlanc / Top 128 in Barcelona RQ / Top 16 Wuhan RO / Top 32 in Singapore RQ / 1 Top 8
- Akali / Performance : / - Top 16 in Barcelona RQ / Top 8 Wuhan RO / Wins Singapore RQ
- With her win in Singapore alongside an improved presence in third-party events, Akali earned a Tier 1 spot she will have to defend going forward. Based on early reviews in Vendetta, it is shocking to think Akali has surpassed Irelia in this set. Following Wuhan, everyone thought the Blade Dancer was back at full force and ready to contend for a third straight set. While Irelia will likely remain a threat, Akali has showed more consistency in regional play, and should be more popular going forward as well.
- Unfortunately, part of that aura has vanished in Singapore, as a top 64 finish places Irelia outside the top 10 legends for that tournament. Combined with Akali winning the whole thing while sporting a similar play style, I expect the Blade Dancer to lose some momentum going forward, unless the Chinese metagame can improve her stock once again.
- Ezreal got a pair of top 16 in Singapore to restore part of the competitive appeal it was slowly losing in the Vendetta metagame. The pace we play Riftbound hasn't changed a lot to explain that rebound for the attrition play style. However, Akali and Irelia now building around Astral Heron (VEN-044) has improved their stock, meaning the top tables are more diverse compared to the fast-paced decks against Kennen and Master Yi we saw in the early stages.
- LeBlanc / Performance : / - Top 128 in Barcelona RQ / Top 16 Wuhan RO / Top 32 in Singapore RQ
- LeBlanc looked to slow to compete early on in Vendetta, limited by an early game built around fairly weak units. With the aggressive decks not being as dominant as expect due to Astral Heron (VEN-044) changing the fortune of midrange Calm legends, LeBlanc is able to enjoy a slower early game in more matches. It seems to suit her a lot more.
