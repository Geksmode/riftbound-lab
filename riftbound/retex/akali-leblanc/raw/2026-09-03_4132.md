## Extraits
- To some extent, the tournament lived up to that moniker with Kennen and Diana, two legends built around playing with a ton of options available at all times, combining for half the top 8. Among the other four, Azir probably is the most aggressive legend compared to Midrange Fiora, Akali and Irelia. Compared to Barcelona's top 8 featuring with Ornn and Rengar, the battle for the win in Wuhan was definitely a slower, more value oriented environment.
- In Barcelona, we already saw Akali beat the odds and make it to a top 16 very few expected her to be able to reach. In Wuhan, Akali used the same recipe to record her first top 8 in Vendetta, Irelia took notice and won the entire event with that same recipe. Up to this point, the Vendetta metagame was built around the ability to conquer consistently, and force the opponent into using all their runes to keep up. The Astral Heron (VEN-044) way emphasizes a lot more on one's ability to hold for the final points of match, dropping the 7-cost directly onto a battlefield while support cards await in our hand, ready to come down for free.
- At the moment, only Irelia and Akali have been using this trick extensively. Nasus played the card first with some success in early third-party events, but failed to transfer that success to regional competitions so far. Going forward, I'm curious to see how many legends might pick up that strategy. Lillia makes sense. Vex played two on its way to a top 32 in Wuhan. Ornn definitely should if not built like the aggro deck from Barcelona. Can we expect Master Yi to do so? Should Azir consider it as a side deck option?
