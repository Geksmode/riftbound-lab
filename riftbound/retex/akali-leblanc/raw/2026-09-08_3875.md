## Extraits
- Abandoning the Kinkou Order and her title of the Fist of Shadow, Akali now strikes alone, ready to be the deadly weapon her people need. Though she holds onto all she learned from her master Shen, she has pledged to defend Ionia from its enemies, one kill at a time. Akali may strike in silence, but her message will be heard loud and clear: fear the assassin with no master.
- After multiple high stakes tournaments in Vendetta, Akali and Irelia do share some similarities, especially in their use of Astral Heron (VEN-044) as the late game anchor. However, Akali has been leaning on the Fury domain a lot more than expected. In doing so, the Rogue Assassin plays more aggressively than the Blade Dancer, especially in the way she develops, or deals with her opponent's board.
- Without the tools available in the Chaos domain to slow down the opponent, Akali has been focusing on cost effective removals and ways to protect her pair of drawing machines : Stellacorn Herder (SFD-048) and Kai'Sa, Survivor (OGN-039) .
- Akali builds around the generic strong cards of the Calm domain, alongside Astral Heron (VEN-044) to beef up the later turns and cheat on the cost of a few cards. The deck packs a ton of draw through Stellacorn Herder (SFD-048) and Kai'Sa, Survivor (OGN-039) while our signature spell Shuriken Flip (VEN-140) has a hefty flow cost. Thus, Akali will always find a way to leverage the ability of the 7-cost.
- As Vendetta progressed, Akali has been building towards a better control of the board, in order to set up to play her fragile 4-cost card in the best possible situation. Typically, that mission will fall to the Fury domain with cards like Falling Star (OGN-029) or Akali, Deadly Weapon (VEN-021) .
- Once set on the board, Akali will switch to support cards in order to derail opposing attempts to remove its units, be it with spells or through a showdown. Long Sword (SFD-022) is great to do so, but the Calm domain plays a much larger role in that part of the game plan, with the usual staples we see in plenty of decks : Defy (OGN-045) , Discipline (OGN-058) or Zhonya's Hourglass (OGN-077) .
- Akali has done nothing but improve in the Vendetta metagame. That same Rogue Assassin everyone considered a weak legend heading into Barcelona. There, a couple of top 16 with a list built around Astral Heron showed Akali had some potential. The following week in Wuhan, a similar deck reached the top 8 to cement that legend as a solid competitive option in high-stakes tournaments. In Singapore, Akali went all the way with an updated list around that same core, featuring Blitzcrank - Impassive as the main innovation.
