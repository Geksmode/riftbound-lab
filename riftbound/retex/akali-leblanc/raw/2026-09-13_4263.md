- The Heart of the Tempest had 118 representatives in Shenyang, 44 more than Irelia in second place in the popularity ranking. Once only 8 players were left, Kennen was still in the lead, with 3 players still standing, now a routine accomplishment for the Heart of the Tempest. Unfortunately, another regular occurrence happened in that Top 8, as Kennen once again dropped the ball and failed to take home the win. This time, the last Kennen lost in the semifinals, leaving Irelia and Akali to battle to decide the first two-time regional winner in Vendetta.
- Akali won that battle, sporting a list awfully close from the winning one in Singapore last week. Now, there is no denying the Rogue Assassin is a top-tier competitor in Riftbound. Sure, she has a terrible conversion rate, with a whooping 2.78% this weekend in Shenyang, as only 2 of the 72 Akalis made it to the top 64. Okay, she doesn't have the best win rate either, with a 45% score across all four regional events so far. When you win, it doesn't really matter to be fair. Especially when you win with the same list twice in a row, and achieve each win in a different region.
- Akali is everything Kennen isn't, a two-time regional winner with subpar metrics. Kennen also is everything Akali isn't, the most reliable legend in Riftbound based on any global metric.
