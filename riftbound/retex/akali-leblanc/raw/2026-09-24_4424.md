## Extraits
- Looking at the rankings, I believe there is some truth in the events played this week. Irelia and Akali won the last three regional events before the bans, and neither suffered a huge setback, so to see them at #1 and #2 feels fine. Neither won anything so far, instead building their rank on the back of many strong performances. Yet, these two did not win a lot of tournament before the balance update either, they just shone in regional tournaments.
- Based on Vendetta history, Irelia is probably a bit shakier than Akali. Even if the Blade Dancer can play without Stacked Deck (OGN-183) , she struggled in the western metagame before the bans and I can't see how that part changes after it. Plus, Vilemaw's Lair (OGN-295) might play a role if some want to target Irelia and Akali this weekend. Sometimes, it's better to be great but not the best, rather than have a giant target on your back.
- LeBlanc and Jayce are a bit tougher to figure out for Los Angeles. Indeed, synergistic strategies tend to do extremely well early on in a new metagame. However, once the rest of the field adapts, they have a tendency to lose a good bit of momentum without the possibility to change a ton in their strategy.
- Overall, the main question to solve heading into Los Angeles is how threatening is the LeBlanc - Jayce duo. These two are the biggest risers following the bans, and joined other legends most expected to be contending after the update. They aren't difficult to edge against due to their synergistic nature. However, doing so limits one's opportunity to focus on raw power, or against another duo, such as Irelia and Akali, or aggressive strategies like Rengar and Rek'Sai.

- Tier 1 LeBlanc / 1 Win / 3 Top 8 / 1 Top 16 / 1 Top 32 / 3 Top 8
- I don't think anyone will question the Blade Dancer's raw power and ability to score if left unchecked. However, the western metagame is typically tough for Irelia, while Vilemaw's Lair (OGN-295) could be a threat if some players decided to target her and Akali.
- Akali / Performance / 64+ Tournaments - 1 Top 8 / 4 Top 16 / 2 Top 32
- As the only legend with two regional crowns alongside not being concerned by the nerf, Akali had little reason to drop in our rankings. However, the Rogue Assassin lost Kennen acting as the scarecrow everyone focused on. Thus, Akali might see a lot more decks prepared to go against her this weekend.
- LeBlanc / Performance / 64+ Tournaments - 1 Win / 3 Top 8 / 1 Top 16 / 1 Top 32
- LeBlanc's best performance comes from a very proactive deck built around Baited Hook (OGN-242) to emphasize on the Deathknell synergy even more. I have no doubt this is a great deck from a raw power standpoint. However, winning a tournament a couple of days after some bans while everyone is figuring out a new list is a lot easier than doing it when everyone has you on their radar.
- The metagame will play a large role in LeBlanc's performance in Los Angeles, but expect her to be in the hunt for a top 8 spot nonetheless.
- Vex drew some attention with a Top 8 in Shenyang, finally building around Astral Heron (VEN-044) like Irelia or Akali do as well. With the bans, the Gloomist still looks like a capable competitor, but relies a bit more on a strong opener, as Stacked Deck (OGN-183) isn't around anymore to find that perfect card when you need it.
- I expected Fiora to do better following the bans, especially as her ability to play different archetypes give her a better chance at a competitive build. LeBlanc and Jayce are both doing great with either Baited Hook (OGN-242) or Dazzling Aurora (OGN-160) as their core. Fiora can build around both gears, but isn't as proactively efficient as the other two when the draws align.
- Viktor remains a large step behind LeBlanc and Azir depending if you compare based on domains or play style. However, the Herald of the Arcanes still managed to be noticed with a top 16 in the largest tournament so far. Last set, Viktor had a surprising run to the Finals of Utrecht. Maybe the Heralf of the Arcanes still has some of that Cinderella magic left.
- Kai'Sa should have done well if the metagame went to a faster pace, as her removal kit is quite good to keep aggressive scorers at bay. Unfortunately, with so much of the Calm domain on top of LeBlanc featuring a deathknell intensive strategy, Kai'sa struggles to keep her opponent in check.
